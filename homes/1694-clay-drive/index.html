Records Don't Happen by Accident

Before hitting the MLS, the listing ran through Compass's Private Exclusive network — giving top agents and their serious buyers a head start. Five brokers brought clients through during this window, creating early demand and a pre-market offer that signaled real buyer interest.

Live on MLS — Maximum Firepower

The listing went live Wednesday evening, layering public MLS exposure on top of the curated private network. The Eichler Social the next day became both a neighborhood event and a live open house — giving the home every possible channel firing simultaneously.

The Eichler Social

Eichler owners are a community. They know who wants a house exactly like theirs. So 150 doors were knocked across multiple Eichler neighborhoods and owners were personally invited to preview the home before it went live. The event doubled as the public open house — combining the energy of an exclusive preview with the firepower of an active listing.
