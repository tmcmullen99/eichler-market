About Clay Drive Eichlers

Los Altos has approximately 50 Eichler homes across the city — the Clay Drive area represents the most concentrated cluster. These late-era Claude Oakland designs (late 1960s–early 1970s) are larger and more refined than earlier tracts, featuring full atriums on generous lots. The neighborhood record of $5,250,000 at 1694 Clay Drive ($2,043/sf) reflects the scarcity premium on this ultra-rare housing type.
