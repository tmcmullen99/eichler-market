About Charleston Meadows

Charleston Meadows spans Eichler's transition from early post-and-beam ranch homes to his more refined mid-period designs featuring larger rooms, improved storage, and more sophisticated atrium configurations. At ~186 homes, it's one of Palo Alto's largest Eichler communities and sits adjacent to the Greenmeadow Historic District.
