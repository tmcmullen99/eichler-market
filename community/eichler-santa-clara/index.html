About Eichler Santa Clara

Santa Clara's Eichler product is unique: the Pomeroy Green and Pomeroy West condo/co-op developments are the only Eichler homes in the city. Built by Claude Oakland in 1961, Pomeroy Green has 78 four-bedroom townhomes. Pomeroy West offers 2, 3, and 4-bedroom condominiums ranging from 950 to 1,550 sf — many single-story units feature atriums, the hallmark of Eichler's design philosophy applied to multi-family living.
