About Fairbrae

Fairbrae and Fairbrae Addition are among Sunnyvale's largest and most sought-after Eichler communities. Protected under the City of Sunnyvale's official Eichler Design Guidelines — a city program specifically designed to help homeowners plan renovations that preserve the neighborhood's mid-century character. Fairbrae is directly adjacent to Rancho Verde, making this corridor the largest concentration of Eichler homes in Sunnyvale.
