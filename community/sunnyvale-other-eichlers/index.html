About Sunnyvale — Other Eichlers

This group spans the full arc of Eichler design in Sunnyvale — from the pre-architect 1949 originals (Sunnyvale Manor I, Sunnymount Gardens) to the late-career large-format homes in Primewood (1700–2300 sf) and Rancho Sans Souci (1800–2600 sf, built 1968). Primewood's double-gabled atrium models, designed by Claude Oakland and Jones & Emmons, are among the largest production Eichlers ever built. All are protected under Sunnyvale's Eichler Design Guidelines.
