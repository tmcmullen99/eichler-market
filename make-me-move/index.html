How Make Me Move works

Your home is always for sale — for the right price. Make Me Move lets you put a number on that without committing to anything.

1
Set your price
Enter the price you'd accept. Buyers see your number — not your name or contact details. Raise it, lower it, or remove it anytime. Never a commitment to sell.
2
Buyers find you
Your home appears in the Make Me Move section of your neighborhood's community page. Buyers browsing Eichler Market see your price alongside recent sales data for context.
3
Handled end-to-end
When a buyer submits an offer, McMullen Properties (DRE #02016832) reviews it, schedules an offer call, and presents it to you — shielding your identity until there's mutual interest.
The offer process

From offer to close.

Every offer follows the same 5-step process — transparent, documented, protected under California law.

1
Offer submitted — LOI drafted
Immediate
The buyer's offer details are formatted into a non-binding Letter of Intent addressed to the home's owner of record per county tax records.
2
Offer review call
Within 24 hrs
A short call to review the offer terms with the buyer, align on price and conditions, and answer any questions — before anything moves forward.
3
LOI signed via Glide
After call
The finalized LOI is sent to the buyer for e-signature via Glide — authorizing McMullen Properties to formally present the offer to you on the buyer's behalf.
4
Offer delivered to you
After signing
The signed LOI is presented directly to you. You see the offer price, terms, and financing — but not the buyer's contact information. McMullen Properties manages the dialogue until there's mutual interest.
5
You respond — on your terms
✓ Accept
A C.A.R. Purchase Agreement is prepared and escrow opens.
↔ Negotiate
McMullen Properties facilitates the counter. You choose to accept, counter again, or decline.
✕ Decline
The buyer is notified through McMullen Properties. You remain private throughout.
About the LOI
The Letter of Intent is non-binding — it formalizes intent, not obligation. All binding agreements use standard C.A.R. forms executed by McMullen Properties, DRE #02016832, a licensed California real estate agent.
