FAQ

Common questions.

What if the home I want isn't listed?
That's the core use case. Submit an offer on any home in the database — including off-market homes. Tim will reach out to the owner through community channels. It takes longer than a standard listing deal (usually 2–6 weeks), but it produces off-market opportunities that don't exist anywhere else.
What's a Make Me Move price, exactly?
It's the number at which you'd sell. Not a listing price — you're not listing the home, and there's no obligation. It's a private signal visible only to buyers who submit formal offers. Set it high. Change it any time. Remove it whenever you want. The only consequence of having one is that you might receive an offer you'd want to say yes to.
Why only Eichlers?
Eichler buyers are a specific type of buyer — they want the flat roof, the post-and-beam, the atriums, the radiant heat. They aren't cross-shopping Cape Cods. Eichler Market works because it concentrates a niche, highly motivated buyer pool around a very specific inventory. That specialization is why 75 serious buyers showed up to one open house in Los Altos.
What's included in the 3% commission?
Full representation on one side of the transaction. For sellers: pricing strategy, Compass Private Exclusive window, MLS timing, community outreach including door-knocking Eichler neighborhoods, the Eichler Social event format, photography, and full close coordination. For buyers: offer strategy, comparable sales analysis, negotiation, and close coordination. One agent, one fee, no dual agency.
Where does the sale history data come from?
The 1,298 home database is compiled from MLS sale records across all Silicon Valley Eichler tracts, normalized and geocoded by tract. Every address, sale price, date, beds, baths, and square footage is sourced from recorded transactions. The data is updated periodically as new sales are recorded.
Who is McMullen Properties?
